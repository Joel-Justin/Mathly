Simultaneous Equations

Simultaneous equations are two equations with two unknowns that are solved together.

Key idea

The same pair of values must satisfy both equations at the same time.

Examples

  • x + y = 10
  • x - y = 2

Quick rule

Use elimination or substitution to find the values of both variables.

Practice

  • Solve x + y = 8 and x - y = 2.
  • Explain why we need both equations together.
  • Compare simultaneous equations with a single equation.
