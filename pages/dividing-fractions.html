Dividing Fractions

Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

Key idea

To divide fractions, flip the second fraction and multiply the fractions together.

Examples

  • 3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 3/2
  • 2/3 ÷ 3/5 = 2/3 × 5/3 = 10/9
  • 5/8 ÷ 1/4 = 5/8 × 4/1 = 5/2

Quick rule

Keep the first fraction, change the sign to multiply, and flip the second fraction.

  • Work out 7/8 ÷ 1/2.
