Exterior Angles of a Polygon

An exterior angle is formed when one side of a polygon is extended outward.

Key idea

The exterior angles of a polygon always add up to 360° when taken around the shape.

Examples

  • Regular hexagon exterior angles are 60° each.
  • A triangle has 3 exterior angles adding to 360°.
  • Exterior angles are formed by extending a side.

Quick rule

Turn around the polygon once; the total turn is always 360°.

Practice

  • Draw a pentagon and mark one exterior angle.
  • Calculate the total of all exterior angles.
