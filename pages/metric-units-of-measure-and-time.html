Metric Units of Measure and Time

The metric system uses standard units like millimetres, centimetres, metres, and kilometres.

Key idea

Metric units scale by powers of ten, which makes conversion straightforward.

Examples

  • 1 m = 100 cm
  • 1 km = 1000 m
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes

Quick rule

Multiply or divide by 10, 100, or 1000 depending on the unit change.

Practice

  • Convert 3.5 m into cm.
  • Convert 2500 g into kg.
  • Convert 2 hours to minutes.
