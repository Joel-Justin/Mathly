Percentage of an Amount

Finding a percentage of an amount means calculating a part out of 100 of the whole.

Key idea

A percentage is a fraction out of 100, so we can divide the whole into 100 equal parts.

Examples

  • 10% of 80 is 8
  • 25% of 60 is 15
  • 30% of 90 is 27

Quick rule

Divide by 100 to find 1%, then multiply by the required percentage.

Practice

  • Find 15% of 200.
  • Find 35% of 80.
  • Explain how 10% helps with other percentage questions.
