Measuring and Drawing Angles

A protractor helps us measure turns accurately and draw angles to a given size.

Key idea

Angles are measured in degrees, and a careful start point makes your drawing accurate.

Examples

  • A 45° angle is half of a right angle.
  • A 120° angle is obtuse.
  • Use a ruler and protractor together for a clean diagram.

Quick rule

Line up the base line with 0° and read clockwise or anticlockwise carefully.

Practice

  • Measure three angles around your room.
  • Draw a 60° angle and a 135° angle.
  • Check that the vertex is in the right place.
