Indices in Algebra

Indices tell us how many times a number or variable is multiplied by itself.

Key idea

The index, or power, tells you how many times the base is used as a factor.

Examples

  • x² = x × x
  • y³ = y × y × y
  • 2⁴ = 16

Quick rule

Write the base and count the repeated multiplication.

Practice

  • Write 5 × 5 × 5 in index form.
  • Evaluate a² when a = 6.
