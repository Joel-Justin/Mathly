Volume of a Cuboid

Volume tells us how much space a 3D object takes up.

Key idea

For a cuboid, volume is found by multiplying length, width, and height.

Formula

  • Volume = length × width × height

Quick rule

Volume uses cubic units because we are measuring three dimensions.
