Symmetry

A shape has symmetry when one half matches the other half exactly.

Line symmetry

If a line can divide a shape into two matching parts, the shape has line symmetry.

Examples

  • A square has four lines of symmetry.
  • A rectangle has two lines of symmetry.
  • A circle has many lines of symmetry.

Quick rule

Fold the shape along a line. If both sides match, it is symmetric.

Key idea

Symmetry is useful for design, art, architecture, and understanding shape properties.
