Exact Calculations

Exact calculations give precise values without rounding away the detail.

Key idea

When exactness matters, leave expressions as fractions, surds, or exact powers rather than decimals.

Examples

  • 1/3 is exact
  • √2 is exact
  • 2π is exact if the problem asks for exact form

Quick rule

Use exact forms whenever the question asks for precision or simplification.
