Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages

These three forms represent the same idea in different ways: part of a whole.

Key idea

1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, so we can move between forms by using equivalent values.

Examples

  • 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%
  • 0.2 = 1/5 = 20%
  • 62% = 0.62 = 31/50

Quick rule

Write the quantity as a fraction with a denominator of 100 to convert to a percentage.

Practice

  • Convert 0.6 to a fraction and a percentage.
  • Convert 3/10 to a decimal.
  • Explain why all three forms are equivalent.
