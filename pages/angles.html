Angles

Angles are formed where two lines meet. They help us describe turn, direction, and shape.

What is an angle?

An angle is the amount of turn between two straight lines that meet at a point. The unit for measuring angles is degrees.

Examples

  • A right angle measures 90°.
  • A straight angle measures 180°.
  • Acute angles are less than 90°.

Quick rule

Use a protractor to measure an angle carefully and check the reference line.

Key idea

Angles are everywhere in geometry, from triangles to buildings to turns in everyday life.

Practice

  • Draw a right angle and label it.
