Comparing Fractions

We compare fractions by finding common denominators or thinking about the size of each part.

Key idea

Fractions with the same denominator are easiest to compare because the numerators tell us which part is larger.

Examples

  • 3/5 is greater than 2/5.
  • 1/2 is greater than 2/5.
  • 3/4 is larger than 2/3.

Quick rule

Convert to equivalent fractions with a common denominator before comparing.

Practice

  • Compare 2/3 and 3/4.
  • Order 1/2, 3/8, and 5/8.
  • Explain why 5/6 is larger than 4/6.
