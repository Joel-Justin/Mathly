Standard Form

Standard form is a quick way to write very large or very small numbers.

Key idea

A number is written as a value between 1 and 10 multiplied by a power of 10.

Examples

  • 3,000 = 3 × 10³
  • 0.004 = 4 × 10⁻³

Quick rule

Count how many places the decimal point moves, and use that as the power of 10.
