Equations & Graphs

An equation tells us two expressions are equal, while a graph helps us picture the relationship between values. Together, they show how numbers change and connect to each other.

Solving equations

Solving an equation means finding the value of the unknown number. For example, in 3x + 5 = 17, subtract 5 from both sides to get 3x = 12, then divide by 3 to get x = 4.

Examples

  • 3x + 5 = 17
  • x - 8 = 10
  • 2x - y = 6
  • x² - 4 = 0

Quick rule

Use inverse operations to undo each step. If a number is added, subtract it; if it is multiplied, divide it.

Graphing straight lines

A linear equation makes a straight line on a graph. The equation y = mx + c has a gradient m and a y-intercept c. For instance, in y = 2x + 3, the line rises by 2 for every 1 step across the x-axis.

Coordinates

The coordinate grid uses pairs like (3, 5). The first value is the x-coordinate and the second is the y-coordinate. Plotting several points helps us draw the graph of an equation.

Practice
