Prime Factorization

Prime factorization means breaking a number down into factors that are all prime numbers.

Example

The number 60 can be written as 2 × 2 × 3 × 5. This is called the prime factorization of 60.

Examples

  • 18 = 2 × 3 × 3
  • 28 = 2 × 2 × 7
  • 45 = 3 × 3 × 5

Quick rule

Keep dividing by prime numbers until only primes remain.

Why it is useful

Prime factorization helps with simplifying fractions, finding common factors, and understanding the structure of numbers.
