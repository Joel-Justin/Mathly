The Coordinate Grid

A coordinate grid lets us describe positions with pairs of numbers.

Key idea

Every point is located by an x value and a y value, written as (x, y).

Examples

  • (3, 4)
  • (-2, 5)

Quick rule

Move horizontally first, then vertically.

Practice

  • Plot the point (2, 5).
  • Describe the coordinates of a point on the y-axis.
  • Explain the meaning of the x-axis and y-axis.
