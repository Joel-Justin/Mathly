Solving Simple Equations

To solve an equation, we find the value of the unknown that makes the statement true.

Key idea

Use inverse operations to undo what has been done to the variable.

Examples

  • x + 6 = 13 → x = 7
  • 4x = 20 → x = 5

Quick rule

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other side too.

Practice

  • Solve x - 9 = 2.
  • Solve 7x = 35.
  • Explain why inverse operations are needed.
