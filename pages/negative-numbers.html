Negative Numbers

Negative numbers are numbers less than zero. They appear to the left of zero on the number line and are used in contexts like temperatures and money.

Number line

The number line helps us compare positive and negative values. For example, -3 is less than -1, and -2 is less than 2.

Examples

  • -5°C is colder than 0°C
  • -2 + 6 = 4
  • 7 - 10 = -3
  • -4 + -2 = -6

Quick rule

When adding negatives, the value becomes more negative. When subtracting, think of moving left on the number line.

Negative numbers in real life

We use negative numbers for debts, freezing weather, and levels below sea level. Understanding them helps us describe change and direction clearly.
