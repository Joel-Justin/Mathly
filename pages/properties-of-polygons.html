Properties of Polygons

Polygons are closed shapes made from straight sides, and their number of sides gives them their name.

Key idea

A polygon can be regular or irregular, and the total of its interior angles depends on the number of sides.

Examples

  • A triangle has 3 sides.
  • A pentagon has 5 sides.
  • A hexagon has 6 sides.

Quick rule

Count the sides and then use the shape name and angle facts to solve the problem.

Practice

  • Name a polygon with 8 sides.
  • Explain why a circle is not a polygon.
  • Classify a regular and an irregular hexagon.
