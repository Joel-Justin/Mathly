Methods of Division

Division is the process of splitting a number into equal groups. It helps us share amounts fairly and calculate how many groups fit into a whole value.

Division as sharing

If you have 24 sweets and share them equally among 6 friends, each person gets 4 sweets. This shows division as equal sharing.

Examples

  • 36 ÷ 6 = 6
  • 84 ÷ 7 = 12
  • 72 ÷ 9 = 8

Quick rule

Division is the opposite of multiplication. If 5 × 8 = 40, then 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

Long division

When numbers are larger, we use long division. We divide, multiply, subtract, and bring down the next digit until all digits have been used.
