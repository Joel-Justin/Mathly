Decimals

Decimals are numbers that include a decimal point and represent parts of a whole.

What is a decimal?

A decimal shows a number in tenths, hundredths, thousandths, and so on. Examples: 0.5, 2.75, and 12.08.

Examples

  • 0.5 = half
  • 0.25 = one quarter
  • 1.5 = one and a half
  • 3.75 = three and seventy-five hundredths

Adding decimals

Line up the decimal points first.

2.5 + 1.25 = 3.75

Quick rule

When adding or subtracting decimals, keep the decimal points aligned and work from right to left.

Practice

  • 4.2 + 2.3 = 6.5
  • 7.8 - 1.4 = 6.4
  • 3.25 + 0.75 = 4.00
