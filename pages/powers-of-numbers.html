Powers of Numbers

A power shows repeated multiplication. For example, 3² means 3 × 3 and gives the value 9.

Square and cube numbers

Squaring a number means multiplying it by itself. Cubing means multiplying a number by itself three times. Example: 4² = 16 and 2³ = 8.

Examples

  • 5² = 25
  • 3³ = 27
  • 10² = 100

Quick rule

The small number tells how many times to multiply the base by itself.

Why powers matter

Powers are used in geometry, scientific notation, and repeated growth patterns. They help us write large or repeated values more efficiently.
