Angles in a Quadrilateral

The internal angles of any quadrilateral always add up to 360°.

Key idea

Knowing that a quadrilateral makes a full turn helps us find missing angles quickly.

Examples

  • Square angles: 90° + 90° + 90° + 90° = 360°.
  • Parallelogram opposite angles are equal.
  • A trapezium has angle totals that also sum to 360°.

Quick rule

Add the known angles and subtract from 360° to find any missing angle.
