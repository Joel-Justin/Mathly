Higher Powers and Estimating Powers

Higher powers show repeated multiplication, and estimating powers helps us make quick sense of large numbers.

Key idea

Powers grow quickly, so they are useful for understanding large quantities and patterns.

Examples

  • 2³ = 8
  • 3⁴ = 81
  • 10² = 100

Quick rule

Estimate by comparing the size of the base and the power before calculating exactly.

Practice

  • Calculate 4³.
  • Estimate 9² without long multiplication.
  • Explain why powers increase very quickly.
