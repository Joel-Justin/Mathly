Properties of a Circle

A circle is a round shape with a centre, radius, and diameter that help us describe its size.

Key idea

The radius goes from the centre to the edge, while the diameter goes across the full circle through the centre.

Examples

  • The diameter is twice the radius.
  • All radii in the same circle are equal.
  • The circumference is the distance around the edge.

Quick rule

Use the centre to measure the radius, then double it for the diameter.

Practice

  • Label the radius and diameter of a circle.
  • Explain why all radii in one circle are equal.
  • Describe the difference between circumference and diameter.
