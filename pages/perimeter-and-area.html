Perimeter and Area

Perimeter measures the outside edge, while area measures the space inside a 2D shape.

Key idea

Perimeter uses length units, while area uses square units because it covers a surface.

Examples

  • Perimeter of a rectangle = 2(l + w)
  • Area of a rectangle = l × w
  • Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height

Quick rule

Use the total of the side lengths for perimeter and square units for area.

Practice

  • Find the perimeter of a 5 cm by 3 cm rectangle.
  • Find the area of a triangle with base 6 cm and height 4 cm.
  • Explain why area uses squared units.
