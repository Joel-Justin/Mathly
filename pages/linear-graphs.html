Linear Graphs

A linear graph is a straight-line graph that shows a constant rate of change.

Key idea

When the variables change by a fixed amount each time, the graph is a straight line.

Examples

  • y = x + 2
  • y = 2x - 1

Quick rule

Use the gradient and intercept to sketch the line.

Practice

  • Plot y = x + 3.
  • State the gradient of y = 2x + 1.
  • Explain why a linear graph is straight.
