Growth and Decay

Growth and decay describe values that increase or decrease repeatedly by a percentage over time.

Key idea

Repeated percentage change uses a multiplier, so the value changes by a factor each time period.

Examples

  • Population growth can be modelled using repeated percentage increases.
  • Decay may happen in medicine, depreciation, or radioactive substances.
  • A multiplier of 1.08 means an 8% increase.

Quick rule

Use a multiplier bigger than 1 for growth and smaller than 1 for decay.

Practice

  • Find the value after a 5% growth over 3 periods.
  • Find the value after a 12% decay over 2 periods.
  • Explain how a multiplier works in repeated change.
