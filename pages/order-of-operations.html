Order of Operations

The order of operations tells us which calculations to do first. Without this rule, the same expression could be interpreted in different ways.

BODMAS

Remember the rule: Brackets, Orders, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction.

Examples

  • 3 + 4 × 2 = 11
  • (5 + 3) × 2 = 16
  • 8 ÷ 2 + 5 = 9

Quick rule

Do brackets first, then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction.

Why it matters

Order of operations keeps calculations consistent. It ensures everyone gets the same answer for a given expression.

Practice

  • Evaluate (7 + 2) × 3.
  • Solve 20 ÷ 4 + 6.
