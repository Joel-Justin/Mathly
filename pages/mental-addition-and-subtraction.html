Mental Addition and Subtraction

Mental methods allow us to add and subtract quickly without writing everything down. These strategies are useful in daily life and in rapid problem-solving.

Round and adjust

To add 49 + 38, we can do 49 + 40 = 89 and then subtract 2, giving 87. This is a useful mental method because it turns the problem into simpler numbers.

Examples

  • 58 + 27 = 85
  • 96 - 38 = 58
  • 72 + 19 = 91

Quick rule

Use friendly numbers and adjust the answer at the end.

Split numbers

Another useful idea is to split a number into tens and ones. For example, 34 + 25 = 30 + 20 + 4 + 5.
