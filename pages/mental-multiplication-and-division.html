Mental Multiplication and Division

Mental methods make multiplication and division faster. We often split numbers apart and use facts we already know.

Break numbers into parts

For example, 24 × 5 = (20 × 5) + (4 × 5) = 100 + 20 = 120. This makes the calculation easier to do in your head.

Examples

  • 18 × 5 = 90
  • 36 ÷ 3 = 12
  • 14 × 4 = 56

Quick rule

Multiply or divide using known facts and split the numbers where it helps.

Using number facts

Knowing times tables and division facts makes mental maths much faster. For example, if 7 × 8 = 56, then 56 ÷ 7 = 8.
