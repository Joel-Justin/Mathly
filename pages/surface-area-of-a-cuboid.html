Surface Area of a Cuboid

Surface area is the total area of all the faces of a 3D shape.

Key idea

A cuboid has six rectangular faces, and the total area of these faces is its surface area.

Formula

  • Surface area = 2(lw + lh + wh)

Quick rule

Work out the area of each face and then add them together.

Practice

  • Find the surface area of a cuboid with sides 4 cm, 3 cm, and 2 cm.
  • Explain why the answer is measured in square units.
  • Compare surface area with volume.
