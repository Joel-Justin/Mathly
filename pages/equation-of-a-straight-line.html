Equation of a Straight Line

The equation of a straight line links the x and y values of the points on the line.

Key idea

In the form y = mx + c, m is the gradient and c is the y-intercept.

Examples

  • y = 3x + 2
  • y = -x + 5

Quick rule

Read the gradient from the steepness and the intercept from where the line crosses the y-axis.

Practice

  • Find the gradient of y = 4x - 3.
  • State the y-intercept of y = x + 7.
  • Sketch the line y = 2x + 1.
