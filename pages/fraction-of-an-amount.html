Fraction of an Amount

Finding a fraction of an amount means splitting the whole into equal parts and taking the required number.

Key idea

Divide by the denominator and multiply by the numerator to find the correct fraction of a whole.

Examples

  • 1/2 of 20 = 10
  • 3/4 of 40 = 30
  • 2/5 of 35 = 14

Quick rule

Divide by the denominator, then multiply by the numerator.
