Angles in a Triangle

Each triangle has an angle total of 180°, which helps us solve missing angles quickly.

Key idea

If two angles are known, the third can be found by subtracting their sum from 180°.

Examples

  • 60° + 70° + 50° = 180°.
  • A right triangle has one 90° angle.
  • An isosceles triangle has equal base angles.

Quick rule

For any triangle, always total the known angles and subtract from 180°.
