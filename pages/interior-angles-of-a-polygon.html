Interior Angles of a Polygon

The interior angles are the angles inside the polygon, and they depend on how many sides the shape has.

Key idea

To find the total of interior angles, use the formula (n - 2) × 180°.

Examples

  • A triangle has (3 - 2) × 180° = 180°.
  • A pentagon has (5 - 2) × 180° = 540°.
  • An octagon has (8 - 2) × 180° = 1080°.

Quick rule

Subtract 2 from the number of sides, then multiply by 180°.
