Multiplying and Dividing by 10, 100, and 1000

Working with powers of 10 is a quick way to change numbers by moving digits place value. This is one of the most important number skills in maths.

Move digits

When multiplying by 10, each digit moves one place to the left. Multiplying by 100 moves digits two places and by 1000 moves them three places. Dividing does the opposite.

Examples

  • 24 × 10 = 240
  • 6 × 100 = 600
  • 540 ÷ 10 = 54
  • 8200 ÷ 100 = 82

Quick rule

Multiply by 10, 100, or 1000 by shifting digits left. Divide by moving digits right.

Using the place value table

A place value grid shows exactly how the digits move. This helps when working with decimals as well as whole numbers.
