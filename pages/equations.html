Equations

An equation is a statement that two expressions are equal.

Key idea

The equals sign shows that both sides have the same value.

Examples

  • x + 4 = 9
  • 2y - 3 = 7

Quick rule

Use inverse operations to find the value of the unknown.

Practice

  • Solve x + 7 = 12.
  • Solve 3x = 18.
  • Explain how an equation is different from an expression.
