Formulas

A formula is a rule written using symbols and variables.

Key idea

Formulas show how quantities are connected and let us calculate unknown values.

Examples

  • Area of a rectangle: A = lw
  • Perimeter of a square: P = 4s
  • Speed: s = d ÷ t

Quick rule

Substitute known values into the formula then calculate the result.

Practice

  • Use A = lw to find the area of a 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle.
  • Write a formula for the perimeter of a rectangle.
  • Explain why formulas are useful in real life.
