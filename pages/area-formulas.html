Area Formulas

Area formulas help calculate the amount of space inside common 2D shapes.

Key idea

Each shape has a formula based on its dimensions and structure.

Examples

  • Rectangle: length × width
  • Triangle: 1/2 × base × height
  • Parallelogram: base × height

Quick rule

Match the shape to the correct formula before substituting values.

Practice

  • Find the area of a rectangle 8 cm by 5 cm.
  • Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.
  • Explain why formulas save time.
