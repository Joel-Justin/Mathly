Properties of Triangles

A triangle is a 3-sided polygon. Its angles always add up to 180°.

Important facts

Triangles can be classified by their sides or by their angles. Some are equilateral, isosceles, or scalene.

Examples

  • An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides.
  • An isosceles triangle has 2 equal sides.
  • A right-angled triangle has one 90° angle.

Quick rule

When you know two angles in a triangle, the third angle is 180° minus their sum.

Key idea

Triangles are the basic building blocks of many other geometric shapes.

Practice

  • Draw each type of triangle.
  • Find the missing angle in a triangle with angles 50° and 60°.
  • Explain why all triangle angles sum to 180°.
