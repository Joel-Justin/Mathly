Multiplying Fractions

Multiplying fractions is a quick way to find part of a part.

Key idea

Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together, then simplify if needed.

Examples

  • 1/2 × 3/4 = 3/8
  • 2/3 × 3/5 = 2/5
  • 4/5 × 1/2 = 2/5

Quick rule

Multiply across top and bottom, then simplify the result if possible.

Practice

  • Calculate 2/5 × 3/4.
  • Calculate 3/7 × 7/9.
  • Explain how simplification changes the final fraction.
