Factors and Multiples

A factor is a number that divides exactly into another number. A multiple is a number in the times table of another number.

What is a factor?

The factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 12, because each of these divides into 12 exactly. Factors come in pairs, such as 3 and 4.

Examples

  • Factors of 18 are 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
  • Multiples of 5 are 5, 10, 15, 20
  • Multiples of 7 are 7, 14, 21, 28

Quick rule

Every number has at least two factors: 1 and itself. Multiples are found by repeated addition of the same number.

Why it matters

Factors and multiples are useful in simplifying fractions, solving number puzzles, and understanding divisibility.

Practice

  • List the factors of 24.
  • Write the first five multiples of 6.
  • Find a common multiple of 4 and 6.
  • Which numbers divide exactly into 30?
