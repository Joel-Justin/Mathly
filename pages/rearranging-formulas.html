Rearranging Formulas

Rearranging a formula means changing the subject of the formula to isolate a variable.

Key idea

Use inverse operations to move terms while keeping the equation balanced.

Examples

  • If A = lw, then w = A ÷ l
  • If P = 2l + 2w, then w = (P - 2l) ÷ 2

Quick rule

Do the opposite operation to both sides, one step at a time.

Practice

  • Rearrange A = lw to make w the subject.
  • Rearrange P = 2l + 2w to make w the subject.
  • Explain why the same operation must be applied to both sides.
