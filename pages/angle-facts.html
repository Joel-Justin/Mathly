Angle Facts

Angle facts help us recognise patterns in shapes and solve geometry problems quickly.

Key idea

Many angle relationships stay the same in shapes, such as right angles, straight angles, and complementary angles.

Examples

  • A right angle measures 90°.
  • Two angles that make 180° are supplementary.
  • Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

Quick rule

Always look for a line, triangle, or full turn that gives you a known angle total.

Practice

  • Find two angles that add to 90°.
  • Explain a straight-line angle fact in words.
  • Draw a right angle and label it.
