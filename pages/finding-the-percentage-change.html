Finding the Percentage Change

Percentage change compares an original amount with a new amount to show how much it increased or decreased.

Key idea

Calculate the difference, then divide by the original amount and multiply by 100.

Examples

  • From 40 to 50 is a 25% increase.
  • From 80 to 60 is a 25% decrease.
  • Difference ÷ original × 100 gives the percentage change.

Quick rule

Always divide the change by the original amount, not the new amount.

Practice

  • Find the percentage change from 90 to 72.
