Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Fractions, decimals, and percentages are three ways of representing parts of a whole. They are closely connected, and understanding these links makes many maths problems easier.

Same value, different form

A fraction like 1/2, a decimal like 0.5, and a percentage like 50% all show the same amount. The key is to remember that percentages are always out of 100.

Examples

  • 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%
  • 3/5 = 0.6 = 60%
  • 7/10 = 0.7 = 70%
  • 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%

Quick rule

To convert a fraction to a percentage, divide the numerator by the denominator and multiply by 100.

Adding and subtracting fractions

You need a common denominator before adding or subtracting fractions. For example, 1/3 + 1/6 = 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2. When the denominators are different, convert to equivalent fractions first.

Finding percentages

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. To find 20% of 80, calculate 20/100 × 80 = 16. Percentages are used in discounts, increases, interest, and test scores.
