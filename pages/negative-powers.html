Negative Powers

A negative power means taking a reciprocal.

Key idea

x⁻n = 1 ÷ xⁿ, so a negative index turns the number into a fraction.

Examples

  • 2⁻² = 1/4
  • 10⁻3 = 0.001

Quick rule

Make the reciprocal and then apply the positive power.

Practice

  • Write 5⁻2 as a fraction.
  • Evaluate 10⁻1.
  • Explain how negative powers connect to fractions.
