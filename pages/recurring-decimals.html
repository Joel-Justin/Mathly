Recurring Decimals

A recurring decimal has a repeating pattern of digits after the decimal point.

Key idea

Some decimals do not terminate; instead, one or more numbers repeat forever.

Examples

  • 1/3 = 0.333...
  • 2/7 = 0.285714285714...
  • 0.121212... is recurring.

Quick rule

Use a dot or a bar to show the repeating block in a recurring decimal.

Practice

  • Write 1/6 as a recurring decimal.
  • Describe the repeating pattern in 0.4545...
  • Explain the difference between terminating and recurring decimals.
