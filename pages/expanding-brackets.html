Expanding Brackets

Expanding brackets means multiplying everything inside the bracket by the term outside.

Key idea

Distribute the outside term across every part inside the bracket.

Examples

  • 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12
  • 2(a + b) = 2a + 2b

Quick rule

Multiply each term inside the bracket by the outside factor.

Practice

  • Expand 5(x + 2).
  • Expand 3(a + b).
  • Explain why the term outside must multiply each inside term.
