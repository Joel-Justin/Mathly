Expressions

An expression is a mathematical phrase made up of numbers, symbols, and operations.

Key idea

Expressions do not include an equals sign, so they are not complete equations.

Examples

  • 3x + 5
  • 2(a + b)
  • 4y - 7

Quick rule

Use order of operations when simplifying an expression.

Practice

  • Simplify 3x + 2x.
  • Write an expression for a number multiplied by 5 then increased by 3.
  • Explain how an expression differs from an equation.
