Rounding and Estimating

Rounding and estimating help us work with numbers quickly and sensibly.

Key idea

Rounding changes a number to a nearby value, while estimating gives a useful approximate answer.

Examples

  • 27.8 rounds to 28 to the nearest whole number.
  • £19.95 is about £20.
  • 48 + 19 is about 50 + 20 = 70.

Quick rule

Look at the next digit: 5 or more means round up; under 5 means round down.

Practice

  • Round 6.47 to one decimal place.
  • Estimate 49 × 21.
  • Decide when an estimate is more useful than an exact answer.
