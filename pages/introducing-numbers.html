Introducing Numbers

Numbers are the basic building blocks of mathematics. They help us count, compare, measure, and solve problems in everyday life.

What are numbers?

Numbers are symbols used to show quantity. We use numbers to count objects, tell time, measure length, and work out money. Examples include 1, 2, 3, and larger values such as 45 or 1,250.

Examples

  • There are 5 apples in a basket.
  • The classroom has 24 chairs.
  • My birthday is on the 7th of the month.
  • 3 + 4 = 7

Quick rule

Numbers can be used to count, order, and measure. Each number has a value and a place in the number system.

Place value

In a whole number, each digit has a place value. In 482, the 4 means 400, the 8 means 80, and the 2 means 2. This helps us understand how big a number is and compare values.

Practice

  • Write the number twenty-six.
  • What is the value of the 7 in 472?
  • Which is larger: 39 or 93?
  • Count forward from 18 to 25.
