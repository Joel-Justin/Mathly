Common Factors and Multiples

Common factors and common multiples help us compare numbers and simplify problems that involve more than one value.

Common factors

A common factor is a factor shared by two or more numbers. For example, the common factors of 12 and 18 are 1, 2, and 3.

Examples

  • Common factors of 12 and 18: 1, 2, 3
  • Common multiples of 4 and 6: 12, 24, 36
  • LCM of 4 and 6 is 12

Quick rule

List the factors or multiples and find the values shared by both numbers.

LCM and HCF

The lowest common multiple (LCM) is the smallest number in both times tables. The highest common factor (HCF) is the largest shared factor.

Practice

  • Find the common factors of 18 and 24.
  • Find the LCM of 3 and 5.
  • Find the HCF of 20 and 30.
  • List the first three common multiples of 2 and 4.
