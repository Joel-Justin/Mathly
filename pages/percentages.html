Percentages

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. The symbol % means "per hundred".

What does percent mean?

50% means 50 out of 100, or half of a quantity.

Examples

  • 25% = 25/100 = 1/4
  • 75% = 75/100 = 3/4
  • 10% = 10/100 = 1/10

Quick rule

To find a percentage, divide by 100 and then multiply by the required percentage.

Key idea

Percentages appear in discounts, marks, taxes, and many real-life calculations.

Practice

  • Write 0.75 as a percentage.
