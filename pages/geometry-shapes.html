Geometry & Shapes

Geometry is the study of shape, size, position, and angle. It helps us understand the world around us, from triangles and circles to buildings, maps, and design.

Angles

Angles are measured in degrees. A right angle is 90°, a straight angle is 180°, and a full turn is 360°. Angles help describe how two lines meet.

Examples

  • Triangle angles add to 180°
  • Quadrilateral angles add to 360°
  • Circle has 360° in one turn
  • Equilateral triangle has 3 equal angles

Quick rule

Keep angle facts in mind: straight angles are 180°, right angles are 90°, and parallel lines create equal corresponding angles.

Polygons and symmetry

A polygon is a closed 2D shape with straight edges. Examples include triangles, squares, pentagons, and hexagons. Many shapes also have line symmetry, where one half is a mirror image of the other.

Triangles and quadrilaterals

A triangle has 3 sides and 3 angles, while a quadrilateral has 4 sides and 4 angles. Different triangles and quadrilaterals have special properties, such as equal sides, right angles, or parallel sides.

  • Name the shape with 4 equal sides

  • Draw a line of symmetry
  • Calculate the total angle sum of a quadrilateral
