How Area Formulas Work

Area formulas come from thinking about rows, columns, or repeated units inside a shape.

Key idea

Area is the number of square units inside a shape, so formulas help count them efficiently.

Examples

  • Rectangles are rows of equal squares.
  • Triangles are half of a rectangle.
  • Parallelograms can be cut and rearranged.

Quick rule

Think in terms of square units and how the shape fits together.

Practice

  • Explain why a triangle formula includes half.
  • Sketch a rectangle and count the square units.
  • Compare formula reasoning with drawing a grid.
