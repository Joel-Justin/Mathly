Percentage Increase and Decrease

A percentage increase or decrease shows how much a value changes compared with the original amount.

Key idea

Find the change, divide by the original amount, and multiply by 100.

Examples

  • £40 increased by 10% becomes £44.
  • £60 decreased by 20% becomes £48.
  • Price rises and falls are both percentage changes.

Quick rule

Always compare the change to the starting value to work out the percentage.

Practice

  • Increase 120 by 15%.
  • Decrease 90 by 30%.
  • Explain the difference between percentage change and percentage amount.
