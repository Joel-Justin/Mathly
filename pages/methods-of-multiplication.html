Methods of Multiplication

Multiplication is repeated addition. There are several efficient written and mental methods that help us calculate accurately and quickly.

Column multiplication

In column multiplication, we multiply each digit carefully and then add the partial products. This method is useful when numbers are larger or when we need a neat written answer.

Examples

  • 12 × 5 = 60
  • 23 × 4 = 92
  • 34 × 12 = 408

Quick rule

Multiply the digits one place at a time and then combine the results.

Useful mental strategies

Sometimes we can multiply by splitting numbers into smaller parts, such as 23 × 6 = (20 × 6) + (3 × 6). This makes mental calculation easier.
