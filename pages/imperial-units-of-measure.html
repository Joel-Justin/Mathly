Imperial Units of Measure

Imperial units are traditional measurements often used in inches, feet, pounds, and miles.

Key idea

The imperial system uses different relationships from the metric system, so conversions matter.

Examples

  • 12 inches = 1 foot
  • 3 feet = 1 yard
  • 1 mile = 1760 yards

Quick rule

Memorise the key conversion facts before solving measurement problems.

Practice

  • Convert 5 feet into inches.
  • Convert 2 yards into feet.
