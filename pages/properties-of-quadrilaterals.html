Properties of Quadrilaterals

Quadrilaterals are four-sided shapes, and each type has different side and angle properties.

Key idea

Knowing the properties of a square, rectangle, rhombus, and parallelogram helps classify shapes correctly.

Examples

  • A square has four equal sides and four right angles.
  • A rectangle has opposite sides equal and parallel.
  • A rhombus has equal sides but not always right angles.

Quick rule

Check the number of parallel sides, equal sides, and right angles to identify the quadrilateral.

Practice

  • List the properties of a parallelogram.
  • Compare a square and a rectangle.
  • Draw a kite and describe its sides.
