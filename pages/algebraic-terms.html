Algebraic Terms

An algebraic term is a number, a variable, or a combination of both.

Key idea

Terms are the building blocks of algebraic expressions.

Examples

  • 3x
  • 5
  • ab
  • 4y²

Quick rule

Like terms have the same variable and power, so they can be combined.

Practice

  • Identify the like terms in 3x + 2x + 5.
  • Explain why 4x and 4x² are not like terms.
  • Simplify 7a + 2a.
