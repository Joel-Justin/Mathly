Angles and Parallel Lines

When parallel lines are crossed by a line, special angle pairs are created.

Key idea

Parallel lines never meet, and the angles they form with a transversal follow matching relationships.

Examples

  • Alternate angles are equal.
  • Corresponding angles are equal.
  • Co-interior angles add to 180°.

Quick rule

Identify the transversal and then match the correct angle pair before solving.

Practice

  • Draw two parallel lines with a crossing line.
  • Label one pair of alternate angles.
  • Explain why corresponding angles are equal.
