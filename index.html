Fractions

A fraction shows a part of a whole. The top number is the numerator and the bottom number is the denominator.

Example: 3/4 means 3 out of 4 equal parts.

Algebra

Algebra uses letters to stand for unknown values. It helps you describe patterns and solve equations.

Example: x + 5 = 12 means x = 7.

Geometry

The angles in a triangle always add up to 180°. This simple fact helps solve many angle problems.

Example: if two angles are 50° and 70°, the third angle is 60°.

Percentages

A percentage compares a number to 100. It is another way to show fractions and decimals.

Example: 25% = 25/100 = 0.25.
